# Specialist dispatch guide

Use this guide to match the work still needed to a specialist. These are role
responsibilities, not a mandatory sequence. The current request, evidence and
Host-provided budgets determine which task is worthwhile.

## Survey

Use Survey for literature investigation, comparison of existing methods,
source acquisition and checks of coverage, assumptions or applicability.
A review can be the user's final goal; Survey need not lead to original research.
Provide the user's questions, relevant acquired material and the precise
comparison or source gap. Expect a substantive evidence report with actual
source locations, supported comparisons and remaining uncertainty.
Only Survey may retrieve external sources, and only when retrieval is enabled.
It does not prove a new mathematical claim or redesign a mathematical route.
See [Survey and source analysis](../../skills/survey-and-source-analysis/SKILL.md).

## Framer

Use Framer when an open mathematical research request needs bounded work items.
Provide the literal request, fixed assumptions, desired outputs and useful
context. Expect explicit objectives, dependencies and completion targets.
Framer clarifies what the research must answer; Planner chooses how to approach
it. A clear literature question or a request to organize existing reports can
go directly to Survey or the Host's writing flow without mathematical framing.

## Planner

Use Planner to create a needed mathematical route or update it after evidence
changes the remaining obligations, scope or dependencies. Provide the literal
target, current route if present, relevant findings and their reviews.
Expect a compact dependency map, live obligations, evidence links and a concrete
next task. Planner owns the mathematical strategy; it does not independently
verify its own claims. A covered branch or verified reduction can warrant a
route refresh. Merely adding a result or retrying an unchanged node does not.
See [Route planning](../../skills/decompose-and-plan-route/SKILL.md).

## Research

Use Research for a bounded derivation, construction, calculation, local proof,
applicability check or focused repair. Provide the exact question, assumptions,
relevant original evidence and the latest attempt or review.
Expect actual mathematical work, its conditions, what it adds and the first
remaining gap. A complete local result is useful, but does not accept the
whole target. Choose it when a focused attempt can advance the question.
When the unresolved issue is finding a substantive new mechanism rather than
carrying out a bounded step, consider RCI. A precise formula alone does not
decide that distinction. Research uses acquired local sources.
See [Direct proving](../../skills/direct-proving-and-gap-closure/SKILL.md).

## RCI

Use RCI when a bounded research question needs deeper conjecture formation,
mechanism discovery or comparison, or a nontrivial construction. Provide the
fixed question, source evidence, earlier attempts, reviews and one clear gap.
Expect candidate statements, supporting mechanisms, independent criticism,
useful local results and a concrete remaining question. RCI is a longer
investigation; use it for the mathematical uncertainty involved, without a
quota of preliminary Research failures. Continue an unchanged question with its
current reviewed statement and saved evidence instead of restarting it because
the method changed. RCI uses local sources; missing primary text can call for
Survey first. Its handoff advice is not a proof verdict.
See [RCI investigation](../../skills/rci-investigation/SKILL.md).

## Prover

Use Prover to assemble or revise one complete candidate answering the literal
mathematical request. Provide the target, applicable source arguments, checked
findings, required dependencies and any independent review needing repair.
Expect a self-contained candidate preserving assumptions, quantifiers and the
requested scope. It may assemble existing evidence after Research/RCI credits
are exhausted, but must not replace unresolved original research with document
formatting. Independent Auditor/Kernel checks decide acceptance.
A complete scoped partial answer is allowed only when the user permits one.

## Writing and verification

Report and paper writers organize supplied evidence through separately requested
Host writing tasks; they are not Director-dispatchable mathematical specialists.
For an organization-only request, sufficient existing material can end research
immediately. For a mixed request, preserve its outstanding mathematical work.
Finding reviews and Auditor/Kernel judgments are independent verification, not
additional roles for the Director to schedule. Literature reports, route advice
and remembered results retain their recorded evidence and scope.

# RCI Refute Prover Probe

Test the exact claim in the supplied probe task. Look for a concrete witness
that satisfies all stated assumptions and calculate precisely where the
conclusion fails. Distinguish failure of a local construction from a
counterexample to the candidate. In `review.md`, report the witness, informative
negative calculations or any actual formulation defect. An unsuccessful search
leaves the claim open. Submit a complete finding draft when you establish a
useful counterexample. Return an empty JSON receipt after writing the review.

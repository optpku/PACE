# rate-and-bound

A rate, bound, constant, lower-bound transfer or resource inversion is required.

Before these checks finish, do not claim this domain obligation is established.

Check the baseline, comparison bridge, legal parameter/region range, recurrence initial value, one-step inequality, endpoint and resource conversion. Preserve inequality direction with variable coefficients.

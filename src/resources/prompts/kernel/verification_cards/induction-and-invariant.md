# induction-and-invariant

The proof uses induction, a recurrence, invariant or localization/continuation.

Before these checks finish, do not claim this domain obligation is established.

Check establishment before use, preservation and continuation, index range and
terminal implication. A local estimate can be proved as a conditional lemma
before containment is closed. Check the safe base and the induction, bootstrap
or first-boundary argument that establishes its conditions when it is applied.
For a boundary argument, verify the needed continuity, boundary attainment and
strict contradiction. Prove uniform bounds separately; instance-only localization
time cannot prove uniform complexity.

Stop if the proof assumes future membership without closing that dependency, or
the base/terminal step is missing. Coupled conditional estimates and continuation
are admissible when the closure is proved. Return the exact missing relation for
Research/Prover repair.

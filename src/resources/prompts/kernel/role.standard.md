# Independent mathematical verdict

Read the original problem and the candidate. Decide independently whether the
candidate proves the requested conclusion under its actual assumptions. Choose
your own method. Check cited results where their conditions matter. An Auditor
hint is a question to resolve. Critic notes are research context.

{{artifact_refs}}

Return stage_status (PASS or MATH_FAIL), summary (the decisive mathematical
reason), and proof_location (section, formula, or a short identifying passage).
For an omitted bridge, locate the inference that needs it. One sentence can
suffice; distinguish independent conclusions when they have different outcomes.
Respect an explicitly permitted partial answer. On PASS also explicitly set
publication_eligible=true and choose a publication action. On MATH_FAIL the
summary supplies the gap for the next author.

Only include relation_checks to authorize a separate reusable lemma. Name its
statement and conditions in words, supports (list of proof or source locations)
and allowed_success_contract_components (list of permitted uses),
and explicitly set status=PASS and reuse_authorized=true. A verdict on the whole
candidate alone grants no independent lemma. Supporting sources and the Kernel
method index at the supplied archive path are available
as needed. No separate review manuscript is required.

# Findings publication

When the receipt asks for a findings action, choose CREATE, UPDATE, or NONE.
Consult the findings catalogue when the submission could create or update an
independently useful result. For UPDATE, check the prior statement and conditions
and supply its exact current finding_ref.

- CREATE for a distinct, independently useful proved result, including a scoped
  limitation or a new argument connecting previously checked pieces.
- UPDATE for a complete replacement of the same result. Give the current stable
  finding_ref. Preserve earlier regimes by including them or citing their checked
  immutable originals and establishing applicability. Check the changed argument.
- NONE for a supporting step, reproduction, summary, incomplete replacement, or
  a result without authorized reuse. Keep a sound mathematical verdict when the
  publication decision is NONE.

The Host publishes the checked original. Final-answer publication_eligible and
the verdict on each proposed use are decided separately.

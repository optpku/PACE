# Calling research

Read the [common dispatch guidance](../dispatch.md)
together with these role-specific instructions.

For `research`, begin with `EXECUTE_LOCAL_TASK`. Select one bounded question,
existing route node or local repair and preserve its assigned objective,
assumptions, scope and expected output. Allow local creative attempts and a
complete proof when feasible. A bounded task may ask what explains a documented
observation. Cite the originating manuscript and request one calculation that
can distinguish the live explanations, or clarify which feature matters when
the explanation is still unknown.

A bounded task may ask whether two documented lines of work constrain the same
choice. Request the first consequence supported by both arguments, a scoped
incompatibility, or the precise missing correspondence. On continuation,
identify the previous joint deduction and the new distinction the next
calculation should resolve.

After an unsuccessful attempt, read the concrete
blocker: a focused derivation or repair may merit Research, unresolved mechanism
or conjecture search may merit RCI, and evidence changing route dependencies may
merit Planner. Do not ask Research to redesign the whole route or automatically
repeat it merely because the same node remains open.


## Versioned task continuity

Use the assigned obligation and its exact input versions. Read the original
statements and assumptions of load-bearing dependencies. Explain the argument
connecting your result to its intended use; do not infer applicability from a
finding identifier or a previous PASS label.

Maintain one complete mathematical manuscript. A separate Reporter extracts the
progress receipt and affected obligation references after this handoff. Do not
write research_delta.json or duplicate the proof in a memory summary.
State separately what was established, what remains unproved, and which change
would make another attempt informative. Independent acceptance belongs to the
designated verifier, not to this handoff.

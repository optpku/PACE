# Calling research

Use `EXECUTE_LOCAL_TASK` for one remaining mathematical relation. In
`prompt_brief`, state the implication to establish or test, any assumptions
or quantifiers that change in this attempt, and the deliverable. Use
`rationale` for why this advances the current target. Cite direct originals in
`input_refs` and name the relevant sections or equations in the brief. Leave
the rest of the route and historical attempts in the archive.

For continuation or repair, name the exact new step and refer to the existing
setup, rather than restating it. For a joint question, name the relation to
prove using both sources. For a literature result, assign the still-missing
application to the present assumptions. Leave the derivation and choice of
method to Research. If the next step requires discovering a new mechanism,
consider RCI instead.

Request one mathematical original giving the new argument and its exact scope,
or the justified obstruction. The Reporter handles the progress update and the
verifier owns independent checks.

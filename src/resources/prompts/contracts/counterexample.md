# Counterexample Contract Writing Guide

Record the literal target identity and all legal assumptions before presenting
the witness. Give the object, parameter choices, domain membership checks, and
the exact failed equality or inequality, with a source or derivation reference
for every nontrivial calculation. Distinguish a local obstruction, an
ill-posedness finding, and a theorem-level refutation of this exact target.

Leave the result open when a check is numerical-only, an assumption is
unverified, or a target clause is untouched. Do not add premises, change
quantifiers, or declare a refutation or completion; the Auditor and Kernel
provide that evidence.

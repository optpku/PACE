# Research Contract Writing Guide

For each proposed direction, bind the claim to one literal target clause, its
legal conditions, the exact source or verified-result references, and one
concrete open obligation. Explain how the direction changes a condition or
conclusion only when that change is permitted by the supplied target scope.
Record source applicability bridges, comparison rationale, and what evidence
would count as progress.

Separate a research direction, candidate, blocked claim, counterexample, and
proved claim in ordinary prose. Do not synthesize a status field, inherit a
neighbor's assumptions, or treat a survey, memory, numerical experiment, or
failed route as proof authority. Keep all unresolved bridges visible.

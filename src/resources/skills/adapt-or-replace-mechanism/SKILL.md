# Adapt or replace a mechanism

## When to use

A source theorem, representation, or proof strategy almost fits, but a hypothesis or intermediate relation does not transfer.

## Inputs

The literal target, exact source passage or current derivation, notation map, and the first failed use. Use actual supplied files; no special mechanism-pack JSON fields are required.

## Procedure

1. Write the downstream relation needed from the source.
2. Match objects and hypotheses, then derive the substituted intermediate steps.
3. Prove missing applicability conditions or change the representation at the actual obstruction.
4. Check that the new conclusion implies the needed relation without changing the target.

## Output and handoff

An insertable adaptation with its conditions, or the first failed transfer and a concrete replacement obligation. Recommend deeper RCI work to Director when the missing ingredient needs exploration. Write into the artifact requested by the role. Cite the source/proof
references and retain unresolved dependencies. Skill text and worked examples
are methods, not evidence for the current problem. Final acceptance still
requires fresh review of the complete literal-target proof.

# Direct Proving and Gap Closure

# Input Contract

- Read the active problem, immutable target contract, and authorized assumption profile.
- Read the canonical Plan as guidance and identify its target-critical bridge.
- Read the selected source passages and mechanism derivations, including the notation correspondence, parameter choices, intermediate derivations, hypotheses at each use, and origins of constants; read verifier-backed relations, rejected relations, and active exclusions from verified progress.
- Read reusable verified dependencies with exact identity, conditions, and authorized conclusion.
- In repair mode, read the fresh canonical blocker and the current-proof verification only.
- Reuse a verified numerical witness only as a search focus and rederive its mathematics.
- Treat Plan steps, Skills, scratch work, examples, and numerical patterns as search context.
- Preserve the target's quantifiers, domain, class, oracle model, and conclusion.
- Fix the ambient spaces, norms or topologies, dual pairings, and meanings of closure, interior, continuity, and convergence used by the target.
- For Research, remain within the one bound blocker and requested local object.

# Procedure

1. Normalize all objects, conventions, indices, parameters, and quantified scopes.
2. Match the proof form to the full quantifier prefix: prove a universal claim for arbitrary admissible objects, prove an existential claim with an admissible witness, refute a universal claim with a countermodel satisfying its residual negation, and refute a pure existential claim with a universal no-witness argument.
3. Write the exact final relation needed for each target clause.
4. Work backward from that relation to the nearest missing intermediate lemma.
5. Work forward from authorized premises with every implication stated explicitly.
6. Meet the two chains at one target-critical bridge rather than hiding the gap in prose.
7. Expand the hardest algebraic, analytic, probabilistic, or combinatorial transition in full.
8. Apply the documented notation and parameter substitutions consistently through the complete derivation.
9. Derive the selected ordered intermediate relations in dependency order, track their conditions, and discharge each condition at use time.
10. Preserve the stated provenance of every imported or derived constant through specialization and assembly.
11. State any supplied construction directly in the proof and prove the properties needed at its use sites.
12. Verify every asserted object exists and belongs to the stated space before using it.
   In composite optimality arguments, verify the specific Fermat membership
   `-grad f(x_*) in partial h(x_*)` before bounding the solution gradient or centering the split;
   the existence of a different bounded subgradient at the same optimum does not establish that
   bound.
13. Do not replace an exact feasible or graph object by an averaged, relaxed, closure, or limit object without a proved recovery, selection, or closedness bridge.
14. For separation, duality, or conjugacy steps, verify the ambient topology, convexity, closedness, qualification, exactness, and attainment actually used.
15. For net or compactness arguments, distinguish convergence from uniform norm boundedness and prove the latter for the exact family or tail used by the extraction.
16. Do not assume that closure preserves a dual-pairing or majorization inequality; prove the required semicontinuity or a structure-specific recovery estimate in the exact product topology.
17. For set-valued graph comparisons, exhibit the common primal point and prove every component membership before summing dual elements.
18. Check base cases, boundary values, degenerate cases, and index endpoints when relevant.
19. Verify constants and signs before telescoping, limiting, optimizing, or specializing.
20. For a recurrence, prove the base value, exact one-step relation, legal iteration range, and solved endpoint bound.
21. For a Lyapunov argument, define the potential, prove its one-step comparison, and connect it quantitatively to the target quantity.
22. For continuation, state the valid region, prove it is preserved, and derive the terminal or stopping implication.
   Audit the dependency order for circular locality: a local smoothness/model estimate may be
   applied only after the exact points and segment are known to lie in its authorized region.
   If the invariant proving that fact is derived from the same estimate, split the step (for
   example with a virtual safe step, a bootstrap region, or an independent bound) and discharge
   the region condition first. Treat “the valid region contains the accepted trajectory” as a
   conclusion unless the target explicitly supplies it as an input premise.
23. Connect every proved intermediate lemma to a downstream target clause.
24. Insert a verified dependency at its exact scope rather than re-proving or broadening it.
25. Preserve currently verifier-backed definitions and relations during a focused repair.
26. Respect do-not-repeat evidence and introduce a substantive new ingredient when required.
27. Classify a failed estimate, recurrence, representation, separation device, or construction as route specific by default.
28. If the bridge is false, expose the mathematical evidence and affected obligations rather than repairing its wording; choose a suitable method within the assignment or propose the needed further work to Director.
29. Reject a target-equivalent intermediate lemma unless an independent upstream derivation is supplied.
30. Bind a standard theorem to an audited source locator or supply its complete local derivation.
31. For a verified witness, reproduce the exact calculation symbolically inside the proof.
32. Assemble definitions, lemmas, constants, cases, specialization, and conclusion coherently.
   For adaptive or rejected-trial methods, include the actual per-trial oracle legality, rollback
   state, acceptance comparison, and a proved count/charging inequality. A prose statement that
   high-curvature trials are charged to a logarithm, displacement, or warm-start term is an
   unresolved mathematical gap until the charged quantity is shown nonnegative, bounded, and not
   reused across trials.
33. Require every final dependency to be proved at the needed scope; an unresolved lemma yields only a conditional conclusion.
34. Before a terminal claim, map each literal target clause to a proved relation; a verified route complaint does not fill an uncovered target clause.
35. For a negative answer, prove that the full requested conclusion fails at literal scope.
36. Recheck that no unauthorized source statement or removed condition entered the argument.
37. Read the emitted artifact end to end and complete every sentence, equation, and final implication.

# Role-Specific Use

- Prover must rewrite one complete standalone proof, including in repair mode.
- Research carries out lightweight local research: new local
  ideas, representations and focused tests are allowed, with full justification
  for any claimed result. Return an insertable derivation, precise partial
  progress or an honest inconclusive result within the assigned question.
- When the missing bridge requires deeper conjecture formation, exploration of
  substantial alternative mechanisms or a nontrivial new construction, report
  the actual attempted relations, their failure and the open research question
  to Director, explaining why SMI would be useful. An exact statement can still
  need this deeper research.
- Research must not repair the whole route, change the Plan, or decide the theorem verdict.

# Handoff and Failure Discipline

- The workflow switches below guide mathematical methods within the assigned
  scope. Recommend work beyond that scope to Director; they do not authorize
  silently enlarging a lightweight local attempt or rewriting the route.
- Switch to boundary exploration when a sign, constant, endpoint, or indexing convention is unclear.
- Switch to counterexample construction when the active relation appears genuinely false.
- Switch to mechanism adaptation when source hypotheses or the route backbone do not fit.
- Switch to object or algorithm construction when the missing bridge is an explicit construction.
- Distinguish a route counterexample from a counterexample satisfying the full target assumptions.
- Treat a route-local MATH_FAIL or incomplete construction as a repair signal, never as theorem refutation.
- Do not present an incomplete bridge, experiment, or failed search as a conclusion.
- In particular, do not accept an adaptive-rate paragraph that says high-curvature or rejected
  trials are "charged" to displacement/logarithmic warm-start terms without a displayed
  nonnegative per-trial charge, disjointness/no-reuse argument, and finite potential bound. Such
  a paragraph is a target-critical unresolved gap, not a proved complexity estimate.
- State the precise unresolved relation if the available context cannot close it.
- Final mathematical authority remains with the complete proof and fresh Auditor/Kernel review.

## Further route guidance

# Method card

**Do not claim:** a target-critical lemma from a renamed target, experiment, source note, or incomplete construction.

Expand the first target-critical bridge from legal premises. Check object membership, conditions, indices, endpoints and downstream use at the point of application. Keep a candidate calculation or source note as context until the general relation is derived; otherwise hand back the exact unresolved relation and its affected conclusions.

**Handoff:** return an insertable derivation or the exact unresolved relation and affected downstream clauses.

**Selected-element guard:** an existential witness and an operation-selected
element need not coincide. Prove a bound for the actual element used by a
universal hypothesis, a valid identity or an independent derivation. For
composite optimality, a bounded member of the subdifferential need not be the
Fermat member that cancels the smooth gradient.

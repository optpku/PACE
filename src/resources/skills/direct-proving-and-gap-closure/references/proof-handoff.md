# Handoff card

Return an insertable derivation or the exact unresolved relation and affected downstream clauses. Keep every condition, scope, and downstream consequence explicit. Until the handoff is checked by the receiving mathematical role, it remains search context.

For partial progress, separate the implication derived from the conditions still
needed at its insertion point. Show the concrete failed equation or construction,
what it rules out and what remains possible. Propose a weaker sufficient estimate,
different representation or missing construction only with its mathematical
purpose stated; return changes to the assigned statement to Planner.

Explain whether the residue supports another lightweight local attempt or needs
deeper research into a conjecture, mechanism or construction. For the latter,
recommend SMI to Director with the actual attempts, the point of failure and the
open research question. Method changes within the assignment are allowed; a
precise route node is not evidence that its mechanism is already available.

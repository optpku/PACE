# Construct Counterexamples

# Input Contract

- Fix the exact assumptions that the witness must satisfy.
- Fix the exact conclusion, relation, algorithm property, or target clause intended to fail.
- Write the formal negation and identify whether it requires one witness or a universal no-witness theorem.
- Record the relevant dimension, parameter range, class, oracle, and method restrictions.
- For a research child, read the inherited immutable core and every explicit condition mutation.
- Distinguish the current route relation from the literal theorem target.
- Read verified obstructions and admissible witness capsules without enlarging their scope.
- Treat an unverified candidate as search evidence until all conditions are checked.

# Procedure

1. Negate the candidate conclusion with its quantifiers and scope made explicit.
2. Follow the full quantifier prefix: instantiate the universal variables and prove the residual negation, or prove universal nonexistence when negating a pure existential claim.
3. Decide whether the task concerns a local route relation or the full requested target.
4. Form an assumption ledger separating inherited, retained, explicitly changed, and removed items.
5. Build a scope table for the challenged route lemma and literal target; include every strict-gap, locality, normalization, topology, and qualification premise.
6. Choose a minimal structured family that can satisfy all active assumptions.
7. Define the candidate object, ambient spaces, topology, dual pairing, parameters, and conventions completely.
8. Verify domain membership and every regularity or class condition symbolically.
9. Verify the witness against the full parent-authorized condition profile, not child prose alone.
10. Verify every premise of the exact challenged relation; a witness to a weakened version does not refute a strict-gap-specific or otherwise strengthened replacement.
11. Verify initialization, oracle information, method class, and transcript restrictions when relevant.
12. Verify randomness, probability, confidence, or adversarial ordering at the required scope.
13. Compute both the challenged route quantity and the literal target quantity exactly rather than relying on qualitative intuition.
14. Show the claimed relation or conclusion fails by a strict, correctly oriented inequality.
15. Check that the failure persists under every convention fixed by the target contract.
16. Identify which child, route, and parent clauses the witness reaches and which it does not.
17. Classify the result as refuted, not refuted, or inconclusive for the stated candidate only.
18. For a theorem-level negative answer, assemble the witness or no-witness theorem into a complete standalone argument.
19. Preserve sibling claims and unrelated mechanisms when the witness is route scoped.
20. Recheck the witness against every literal target clause before claiming full coverage.
21. Record exact equalities and inequalities so the failure can be independently reproduced.

# Handoff and Failure Discipline

- Hand off a route-specific violation to mechanism replacement or direct route revision.
- Hand off a fully admissible literal witness to complete negative-proof assembly.
- Hand off a near-witness with one unmet condition to object construction or boundary exploration.
- If the witness exposes a necessary condition, state the exact neighboring profile tested.
- No counterexample found is not evidence that the claim is true.
- Failure to construct a requested witness is not a proof that no witness exists.
- Failure of one ansatz is not failure of every possible algorithm or construction.
- Distinguish an obstruction to one rate from impossibility of every weaker guarantee.
- A toy violation outside the active class is diagnostic, not a mathematical refutation.
- A verified weakened child statement is not a parent refutation without the parent scope bridge.
- Preserve the exact condition profile when exporting a verified boundary to research.
- Do not broaden a verified witness beyond its dimension, oracle, randomness, or condition scope.
- Only fresh ordinary verification can turn the completed negative proof into a terminal result.

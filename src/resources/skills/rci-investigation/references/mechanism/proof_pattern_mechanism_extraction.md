# Mechanism Skill: Proof-Pattern Extraction

Use only proof patterns visible in supplied source refs. Identify the source
mechanism object, the step that fails or becomes nonuniform in the candidate's
setting, and the smallest lawful adaptation that could yield an intermediate.

Separate the nonroutine mathematical move from induction, exact unrolling,
definition expansion, notation, typing, and bookkeeping. Map the result to the
proposal's statement, inputs, output, dependency IDs, proof object, and support
boundary. Preserve every source hypothesis and leave an applicability bridge
open unless it is derived from anchor assumptions.

Do not infer a source pattern from the seed wording alone, invent a source
theorem, or extract a bridge that assumes the desired conclusion.

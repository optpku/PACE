# Raw Seed: Contribution Boundary

Explain the seed's nontrivial value to the current unresolved mathematical
objective separately from its literature novelty. Discovering a mechanism for
an existing conjecture, resolving a difficult applicability bridge, or finding
a useful construction or obstruction can justify deeper research without
proposing a new theorem statement.

Use `closest_boundary` and `minimal_delta` to locate what remains beyond the
available evidence. Subtract definitions, notation, standard bookkeeping,
routine applications and generic motivation. If source novelty is uncertain,
record a specific uncertainty without abandoning a mathematically valuable
question or claiming novelty. A fully supplied argument that already closes
the assigned obligation leaves no unresolved research merely because it has
not been repackaged.

Do not inflate a low-value lemma, diagnostic package, or routine extension.
An existing candidate mechanism may be recorded as a conjectural lead. Do not
insert it as an established premise or require later exploration to preserve it.

# Raw Seed: Route Origin

Classify the mathematical pressure that produced the seed only when that helps
explain its `minimal_delta`. Useful origins include an explicit source
limitation, a localized bottleneck, an assumption transfer, a rate or resource
gap, a geometry mismatch, or a concrete failure case.

Choose the origin that is visible in the research question, closest boundary,
minimal delta, and failure tests. The label is explanatory only; it is not a
Host routing field and need not be emitted as a separate artifact. Reject a
generic “generalization” label with no named mathematical boundary.

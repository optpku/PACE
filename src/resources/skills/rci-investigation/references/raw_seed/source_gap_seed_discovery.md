# Raw Seed: Source-Gap Discovery

Develop one bounded research question from the selected direction or supplied
exact conjecture. Use the supplied sources and earlier attempts to locate the
unresolved mathematical work. A raw seed frames that question; it does not
establish a theorem or prescribe a mechanism that later roles must accept.

1. Identify the closest established result or explicit limitation visible in
   the supplied materials; record absent source coverage honestly.
2. Record its assumptions, conclusion, scope, and relevant mathematical move.
3. State the smallest unresolved obligation inside the selected work item. For
   an existing conjecture or route node, this can be its missing proof mechanism
   or nontrivial applicability bridge; do not manufacture a new statement.
4. Explain why the visible evidence does not already settle that obligation.
   Distinguish a known statement from an available proof or transfer at the
   required scope.
5. Name one local bottleneck, one toy case, and at least two falsification
   tests.
6. Record missing source coverage as uncertainty.

Prefer a question with a concrete unresolved mechanism, boundary, rate,
construction, obstruction or applicability issue. Separate its value to the
current research from any claim of literature novelty. Avoid source paraphrases,
routine applications and broad requests to generalize. Preserve a supplied
candidate mechanism as a contestable lead, without making it a guaranteed
ingredient of the seed.

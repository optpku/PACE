# Raw-Seed Frontier Readiness Attack

Check whether Frontier Explorer can formulate a bounded candidate without
inventing the seed's question or being forced to accept a premature route.
An already precise conjecture or Planner node can be that candidate; its
supporting mechanism need not be mature before RCI begins.

The seed should expose a precise research question, mathematical object,
closest boundary, minimal delta, one local bottleneck, copied legal assumptions,
a toy case, and at least two concrete failure tests. Source uncertainty may
remain explicit.

Block readiness when the object is only a topic, the bottleneck restates the
entire desired result, assumptions hide a certificate or conclusion, no
repeatable test exists, or the seed requires a proposed mechanism to be accepted
as established. A Planner's mechanism offered as a contestable starting point
is legitimate research context. The seed may preserve the caller's exact
question while identifying its first unresolved bridge, without inventing a
new `minimal_delta` to pass review. Readiness is a handoff judgment, not a proof
or novelty verdict.

# Joint Support And Frontier Audit

Audit the candidate and its selected support without pretending the support is
a proof. Replay every selected mechanism from exact anchor assumptions and stop
at the first incorrect or circular step. Check compatibility across domains,
quantifiers, constants, and endpoint regimes. Independent mechanisms need not
compose into one terminal node.

Then audit the candidate itself: close all definitions and quantifiers, match
assumptions to the anchor, verify positive/refutation duality, and run a concrete
in-scope counterexample calculation. Failure of a proof route is not a
counterexample to the target.

Require a bounded, nonroutine mathematical candidate with substantive support
and an honest open target. Definition expansion, notation, type/domain
normalization, and adapters are not mathematical results. A fresh prover's
inability to find a first step is allowed.

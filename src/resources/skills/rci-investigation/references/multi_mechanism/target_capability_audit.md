# Candidate Frontier Audit

Audit the candidate's mathematical requirements independently of which
mechanisms happen to be available.

1. Reconstruct the claim shape from the reviewed seed and frozen anchor.
2. Check every candidate assumption against the anchor.
3. Ask whether granting an alleged primitive would already solve a substantial
   part of the target; if so, it is not primitive.
4. Identify the indispensable nonroutine assertions and ensure the candidate
   remains bounded and checkable.
5. Remove definitions, notation, typing, domain bookkeeping, and direct
   substitutions from the substantive mathematical task.
6. Check statement, formal context, scope, quantifiers, positive/refutation
   duality, value, and checkable partial progress.
7. Split independent valuable targets rather than combining unrelated claims.

Do not accept a polished record when the target is false, undefined, trivial,
outside the work item, or stronger than its legal assumptions support.

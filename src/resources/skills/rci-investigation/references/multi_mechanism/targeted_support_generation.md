# Targeted Partial-Support Generation

Start from a lightweight support brief, not a demanded final capability
signature. Produce one mathematically correct intermediate that materially
helps attack the candidate while leaving the target open.

Use only anchor assumptions and exact accepted predecessor outputs. The new
output must not restate the target, an unproved target assertion, or a definition/type
adapter. It may be independent of existing mechanisms. Record an honest support
boundary and a concrete positive and hostile test. Stop once the candidate has
substantive partial support; do not generate mechanisms until full coverage.

# Direct Child-Context Projection

Project one accepted substantive-partial candidate into a prove-or-refute child
task without changing its mathematics.

1. Copy the final statement, formal context, scope, positive target, and
   refutation target exactly.
2. Copy only legal assumptions from the frozen anchor.
3. Include selected promoted mechanism refs only as optional search context.
4. Keep mechanism outputs at candidate level; never promote them to assumptions
   or proof.
5. Keep mathematical validation checks in the rich child context but exclude
   them, mechanisms, and sources from mechanism-free prover and dedup views.

Projection may expand an existing definition or align notation mechanically. It
may not introduce a mathematical move, assumption, hidden proof, source claim,
coverage receipt, or Challenger artifact.

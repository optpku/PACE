# Mechanism Support Composition

Compose only the exact promoted mechanisms and their Critic receipts for the
current candidate. The composition records reviewed partial support, not a proof
certificate.

Make frozen anchor assumptions available at entry. For every dependent input,
name an earlier selected producer and copy the producer's output tuple exactly.
Independent mechanisms may appear together without feeding one terminal node.
Choose one representative mechanism for packaging only; it receives no extra
mathematical authority.

The candidate target remains the closure reference for the assessment. No
mechanism output may equal the candidate target or an unproved target assertion.
Definition expansion, notation alignment, typing,
domain bookkeeping, and direct substitution are routine projection and do not
justify another mechanism.

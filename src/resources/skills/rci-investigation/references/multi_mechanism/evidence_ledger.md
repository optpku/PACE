# Multi-Mechanism Evidence Discipline

Ground central mathematical claims without confusing search history with source
evidence. For each claim used by the current role, record a stable local ID, the
exact claim, its status, direct source or predecessor refs, the visible
calculation or proof-pattern basis, the fields it supports, and its limits.

Useful statuses are `SOURCE_PROVEN`, `SOURCE_GAP`, `PATTERN_INFERRED`,
`DIRECTLY_DERIVED`, `CONJECTURAL`, `LITERATURE_RISK`, and `UNSUPPORTED`.
Use `DIRECTLY_DERIVED` only when the derivation is visible in the supplied
inputs or current output. Mark candidate targets `CONJECTURAL`. Prior attempts
may guide search but are not source support.

Evidence notes may support seed, candidate, mechanism, or coverage reasoning.
They must not enter direct child contexts, prover views, or dedup views, and
they never create proof authority.

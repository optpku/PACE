# Calling survey

Read the [common dispatch guidance](../director-dispatch/SKILL.md)
together with these role-specific instructions.

For `survey`, name one bounded prior-result check or source need and request
the relevant sources, their coverage and remaining applicability gaps.

A bounded source need may be phrased by its use in the argument. Identify the
effect requested and ask for a proof passage, its assumptions and the first
transfer gap.

Comparing the assumptions, conclusions and coverage of supplied lectures or
papers is also Survey work, including when external retrieval is disabled.
There need not be a missing document. Distinguish that comparison from a new
derivation needed to establish a claim the supplied material does not support.

Use Orientation's account of the requested outcome to choose the scope. A
source-only review may be satisfied by checked literature and an honest account
of source limitations; a request for a new mathematical result may need further
research after the source check. Ask Survey to make the actual coverage and
unresolved source needs clear in its existing report, without creating new
receipt fields or claiming mathematical verification.

# Seed Evidence Discipline

Distinguish the status of every source-bearing seed claim. Useful statuses are
`SOURCE_PROVEN`, `SOURCE_GAP`, `PATTERN_INFERRED`, `DIRECTLY_DERIVED`,
`CONJECTURAL`, `LITERATURE_RISK`, and `UNSUPPORTED`.

For a source-supported claim, name an existing supplied source ref and state the
exact visible theorem, limitation, or passage plus what it does not establish.
For a direct derivation, show the calculation from supplied fields. Mark the
research question, minimal delta, and unresolved bottleneck conjectural unless
the inputs establish them. A prior model assertion, filename, memory note, or
lack of a search result is not evidence.

Keep evidence notes compact and inside the role's reasoning fields. Do not add a
new lifecycle object or copy evidence into the target anchor or legal
assumptions.

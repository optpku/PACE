# Raw Seed: Attack Surface and Failure Tests

Make a seed falsifiable before frontier construction. State one local
`bottleneck`, one explicit `toy_case`, and at least two distinct
`failure_tests`.

A useful bottleneck names the first nonroutine inequality, construction,
transfer, obstruction, endpoint, or classification boundary. “Prove the
theorem” is too broad. A toy case fixes enough data to permit an actual
calculation. Each failure test names the knob to vary, the observable to
compute, and what outcome would break or narrow the direction.

Failure tests pressure the seed; they are not a proof plan and do not establish
truth when they survive.

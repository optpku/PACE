# Raw Seed: Claim-Shape Protocol

Use `research_question`, `mathematical_object`, `minimal_delta`,
`legal_assumptions`, and `target_clause_ids` to expose the seed's mathematical
shape while distinguishing the assigned conjecture from an established theorem.

The mathematical object names its ambient space, domain, parameters, and
quantifiers when known. The research question identifies what is to be
controlled, compared, constructed, classified, bounded, or refuted. The
minimal delta identifies the unresolved work beyond the available evidence and
stays inside the work item. For a supplied exact conjecture or route node,
preserve the question and use that field to locate its missing mechanism or
bridge; a new theorem statement or claim of novelty is not required. Copy every
legal assumption and target-clause ID exactly from the frozen inputs.

Do not hide the desired conclusion, a certificate, an anticipated mechanism
output, or a stronger premise among the assumptions. If a definition remains
unsettled, state that uncertainty rather than pretending it is fixed.

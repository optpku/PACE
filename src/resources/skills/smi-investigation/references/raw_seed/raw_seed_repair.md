# Raw Seed: Critic-Guided Repair

Use this Skill when Seed Scout receives explicit mathematical and research
review briefs for the same seed revision. The Critic diagnoses defects; Seed
Scout owns the repaired seed.

Preserve the closest valid in-scope direction. Repair the research question,
mathematical object, closest boundary, minimal delta, bottleneck, toy case,
failure tests, source refs, or uncertainties only as required by the briefs.
Keep legal assumptions and target-clause IDs copied verbatim from the work item.

Do not paste the Critic's prose, broaden the target to escape an objection, add
a premise, claim that a source gap is solved, prescribe a downstream mechanism,
or turn the seed into a theorem or proof plan. If no honest bounded repair
exists, preserve the blocker so the next review can reject the route.

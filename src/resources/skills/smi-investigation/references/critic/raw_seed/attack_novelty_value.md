# Raw-Seed Research-Value Attack

Assess value to the current unresolved objective and literature novelty
separately. Compare the seed's `minimal_delta` with the supplied evidence by
object, assumptions, quantifiers, controlled quantity, conclusion, scope,
failure modes and proof object when visible.

Treat the seed as directly covered only when the supplied evidence actually
closes its mathematical obligation under matching scope, including any needed
nontrivial applicability bridge. An existing precise conjecture or known
statement can still need a difficult mechanism. Broad topical similarity is
not duplication. When source detail is insufficient, record the exact
uncertainty rather than claiming either coverage or novelty.

Subtract definitions, notation, routine applications, exact unrolling, standard
comparison bookkeeping and generic motivation. The remainder must contain a
concrete nontrivial research obligation, such as a missing mechanism,
construction, obstruction or transfer argument. Reject a source paraphrase,
routine theorem application, diagnostic-only packaging or fully solved route.
Do not reject useful research solely because its statement is already fixed or
literature novelty has not been established.

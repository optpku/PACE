# Raw-Seed Counterexample Attack

Construct the smallest explicit instance that pressures the seed's mathematical
direction. Generic advice to check examples is insufficient.

Specify the instance, parameters, dimension or horizon, and why every legal
assumption is satisfied. Calculate a named observable such as a slack sign,
residual, equality case, coefficient gap, divergent constant, lost
cancellation, noise floor, or endpoint instability. Say whether the calculation
breaks the direction, narrows it, or becomes a reusable failure test.

A seed survives this attack only when the explicit calculation does not break
it and the calculation still exposes a useful downstream test. No
counterexample found without a visible instance and calculation is
inconclusive, not acceptance.

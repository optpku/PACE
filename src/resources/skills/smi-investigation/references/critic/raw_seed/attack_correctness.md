# Raw-Seed Correctness Attack

Extract a precise necessary implication or in-scope test of the seed's question.
For an assigned exact conjecture, preserve its statement: a simpler diagnostic
test helps examine it but does not replace the research target.

1. Identify the mathematical object, ambient setting, quantifiers, and copied
   legal assumptions.
2. Attempt the first nonroutine algebraic, logical, geometric, probabilistic,
   or source-to-target step.
3. Name the first resulting obligation and check that it does not assume the
   desired conclusion, certificate, convergence statement, or missing bridge.
4. Test an equality, endpoint, singular, zero, or limiting case relevant to the
   stated scope.

A coherent local attempt need not prove the seed. Block a changed target,
unstated assumption, circular implication, undefined object, impossible regime,
or source boundary used only as decoration. State the exact calculation or gap
in the Critic finding.

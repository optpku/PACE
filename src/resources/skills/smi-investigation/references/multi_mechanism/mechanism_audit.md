# Mechanism Mathematical Audit

For a joint mechanism, perform three blocking attacks:

1. Correctness: execute the first nonroutine derivation from declared inputs and
   identify the first algebraic, logical, domain, sign, or assumption failure.
2. Counterexample: calculate a named observable on a positive and hostile
   in-scope instance.
3. Interface and provenance: verify every primitive, dependency output,
   provided capability, and support-boundary claim against the actual move.

Reject circular use of the target or an unproved target assertion. A complete schema is
not evidence of truth. Conversely, novelty, standalone value, and difficulty
are not blocking requirements: a routine or known mechanism promotes when it
is correct, honest, and concretely useful to the candidate. Coverage separately
judges whether the selected set gives substantive nonredundant support.

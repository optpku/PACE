# Mechanism Skill: Core Mathematical Move

A mechanism is a precise candidate-relevant intermediate, not a theorem package
or proof verdict. It may be an inequality, invariant, certificate,
decomposition, recurrence, comparison, localization, coupling, or obstruction.

A complete proposal identifies:

- a compact mathematical `statement`;
- exact `inputs` copied from anchor primitives or accepted dependency outputs;
- the intermediate `output` established by the move;
- direct `dependency_mechanism_ids` and only the supplied `source_refs` it uses;
- a visible `proof_object` or derivation;
- an honest `support_boundary` saying which part of the candidate remains
  unsupported.

Execute a positive and hostile check. Reject a proposal that assumes its own
output or the candidate target; merely postulates a needed
certificate; or advertises routine algebra as the main move. The mechanism may
be useful without being novel or difficult, but it must be correct and
noncircular. The Host assigns the mechanism ID after proposal.

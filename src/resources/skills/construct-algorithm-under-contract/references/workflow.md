# Construct an Algorithm in Proof Prose

# Input Contract

- Read the literal target, the current dossier, the selected source or mechanism context, and the
  other injected Skills.
- Treat Plans, Survey material, prior proofs, prototypes, numerical results, and local workbench
  notes as SEARCH_CONTEXT_ONLY.

# Procedure

The algorithm and its success semantics live in the proof prose. Do not create a separate
machine-readable contract, construction file, certificate, or lineage record. State the instance
class, legal oracle interface, inputs and outputs, initialization, computable parameters,
transitions, branches, restarts, stopping, edge cases, auxiliary sequences, potentials, trajectory
safety, rate, complexity, and sharpness requirements that the literal target actually requests.
Classify quantities as literal inputs, oracle observations, theorem data supplied by the instance,
or proof-only values. Unknown optima, future iterates, and solution-dependent constants are never
computable merely because the proof names them.

Prove every requested property in the current scope. Establish legality before first use,
well-definedness, invariant or potential, all accepted and rejected trials, restart and oracle
accounting, finite-horizon and boundary cases, and the exact target assembly. Keep upper-bound and
lower-bound reasoning separate. A special case, pseudocode fragment, experiment, or failed method
does not settle a broader target.

If the target is false or ill-posed, give a target-relative witness or incompatibility and verify
all literal hypotheses and quantifiers. A local route failure is only repair evidence. Finish with a
complete proof or a precise negative resolution covering every literal target clause.

# Handoff and Failure Discipline

- Planner records obligations and context files; it does not choose a construction for the Prover.
- Prover writes one self-contained proof and owns the mathematical route.
- Research studies one requested property as search context and never replaces the proof.

Describe missing work in ordinary prose and point to the relevant context path. The Host selects
files, assembles the prompt, invokes the model, persists the raw response, and enforces only literal
target identity and final Auditor/Kernel evidence. It does not normalize, score, or materialize
mathematical contracts or artifacts.

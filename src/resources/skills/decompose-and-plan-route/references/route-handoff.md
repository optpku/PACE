# Handoff card

Return the next scoped obligation with dependencies, falsifier, supporting
evidence and the mechanism that remains unknown. Keep every condition, scope,
and downstream consequence explicit. Until the handoff is checked by the
receiving mathematical role, it remains search context.

Budget against the Host's current proof-state balance: Research and Prover each
consume one per dispatch, including partial or failed attempts. Other roles,
Auditor/Kernel checks and reporting do not consume proof-states, although they
consume time and compute. Allow for final Prover assembly and planned
Research/Prover repairs; do not reserve separate Auditor or Kernel slots.

Give the next ready node its candidate relation, proposed operation and reason
for advancing a target branch. Preserve unaffected branches. If a route's strong
bridge failed, state whether a weaker relation would serve the consumer before
discarding the target or changing its assumptions.

Explain whether the next work is a lightweight local attempt for Research or
deeper, longer investigation for SMI into a conjecture, mechanism or construction.
Existing precise nodes can need SMI. Give the mathematical reason rather than
equating a formula with readiness. Recommend canonical route revisions separately;
Director chooses dispatch and Planner retains route ownership.

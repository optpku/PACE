# Decompose and plan a route

## When to use

A target needs a dependency-aware route, or review has invalidated a step of the current route.

## Inputs

The literal target, available source/proof artifacts, current conditions, and latest mathematical blockers. Use actual supplied files; no special mechanism-pack JSON fields are required.

## Procedure

1. Work backward from each target clause and forward from established premises.
2. Choose the weakest sufficient bridge and expose its independent derivation obligation.
3. Put conditions and legality before their uses; identify a concrete falsifier for uncertain steps.
4. Preserve unaffected branches and recommend a bounded next task with its inputs and expected result.

## Output and handoff

One coherent route with proved, conditional, and unresolved steps distinguished. Director dispatches; the plan does not authorize assumptions or decide mathematical acceptance. Write into the artifact requested by the role. Cite the source/proof
references and retain unresolved dependencies. Skill text and worked examples
are methods, not evidence for the current problem. Final acceptance still
requires fresh review of the complete literal-target proof.
